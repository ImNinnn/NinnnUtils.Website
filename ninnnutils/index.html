Fine print

Terms & privacy

Kept short on purpose. If it's not written here, I'm probably not doing it.

Terms of Service

By adding NinnnUtils to your server, you agree to the following:

  • Don't use the bot to exploit glitches or bugs.
  • Don't use the bot to organize or assist in server raids.
  • Follow Discord's own Terms of Service while using it.

That's the whole list. Play fair and enjoy the features.

Privacy Policy

What gets collected

Basic Discord identifiers - user IDs, server IDs and channel IDs - stored locally to keep your settings intact across bot restarts. Message content is cached in memory for up to two hours then dropped for good. Anything you save with /settings or /notes is stored too.

How it's used

  • IDs power economy balances, levels, leaderboards, boards, and TOS enforcement.
  • Cached messages power ghost-ping alerts, boards, and the edit/delete lookup commands.
  • Settings and notes exist purely so the bot remembers your preferences and lists.

Who sees it

Nobody but me. I'm the only person with access, and live message content isn't monitored under normal use.

Deleting your data

Join the support server and ask in the data-removal channel - everything tied to your profile gets wiped.
